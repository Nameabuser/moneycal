# MoneyCal

A simple, fast money tracker that runs on your phone like a real app. Track what you spend, where you spend it, and who owes you money.

## Features
- Log spending with amount, place, category and date
- Monthly overview and spending by category
- Track money owed to you and money you owe
- Save regular people once and tap to fill their name
- Dark and light mode
- Backup and restore your data
- Works offline, no account, no ads

## How to use
1. Open the site in Safari on iPhone
2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen
3. Keep "Open as Web App" on

## Privacy
All data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.
